Settings

Defaults for new scans and renders. Existing jobs keep the settings they started with.

Powers the LLM dialogue and vision signals used by the Thorough preset. Leave blank to turn both off. On Umbrel the Ollama app is reachable at http://ollama_ollama_1:11434.

Measured here: llama3.2:3b runs about 2.4x faster than llama3.1:8b for this job. Larger models are not worth it on a CPU.

Roughly 29 seconds per shot with moondream on four CPU cores, so the Thorough preset's vision pass adds hours. Bigger vision models are far slower still -- one 8B model measured 0.3 tokens per second here.

Automatic uses Apple hardware on macOS and selects 10-bit HEVC for HDR sources.

720p is recommended even for 4K sources. Edit timestamps and final output retain the original timeline and resolution.

Full verification catches damaged or undecodable frames before the output is accepted.

Use an absolute path. On macOS this can be a folder under /Volumes or your home directory. Blank keeps output in CleanCut's data folder.
